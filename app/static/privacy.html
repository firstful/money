Privacy Policy

Effective: August 20, 2026 · App: money.ffilt.us

This app is a personal money-flow visualization tool ("the Service"). It lets the account owner import transaction data and connect financial accounts through Plaid to view spending and money movement. This policy describes what data is processed, why, how long it is kept, and how it can be deleted.

1. Data we process

  • Transaction data — imported bank/credit-card/merchant transaction records, and bank account transaction data retrieved via Plaid's APIs.
  • Account metadata — names and identifiers of the Plaid-linked financial accounts, and their access tokens, stored so the Service can sync transactions.
  • Uploaded files — the CSV files you import (e.g. bank statements, order histories).

The Service does not require, collect, or store your bank login credentials. When you connect through Plaid Link, you authenticate directly with your bank, and your credentials never reach this Service.

2. How data is used

Data is used solely to operate the Service and produce the requested money-flow views and correlations. It is not sold, shared, or used for advertising.

3. Third-party processing (Plaid)

Transaction data for connected accounts is retrieved through Plaid (plaid.com/legal). Plaid processes data on our behalf in accordance with its own privacy and data policies. You can disconnect any linked account from the Service at any time, which stops further retrieval from that account.

4. Storage & security

Data is stored on the account owner's private, self-hosted infrastructure. Access to the Service is protected by multi-factor authentication, service tokens, and role-based access controls. See our Access Control Policy and Information Security Policy for details.

5. Retention & deletion

Please see our Data Retention & Deletion Policy for retention windows. You may request full deletion of any or all of your data at any time (see Section 7).

6. Your choices

  • Disconnect accounts: remove a Plaid-linked account at any time.
  • Delete data: request deletion of uploaded files, transactions, or the entire dataset.

7. Contact & deletion requests

To request deletion or ask any question about your data, contact the Service operator (reedsutliff@gmail.com).

This Privacy Policy applies to the Service as of the effective date above and supersedes prior versions.
